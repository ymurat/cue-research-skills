# Provenance and reuse notes

These templates were newly drafted as generic, publisher-neutral scaffolds after a structural review of a private archive of historical academic work and submission materials supplied by the repository maintainer.

The review used only high-level section-heading patterns from LaTeX source files and structural signals in selected PDF submission/revision materials. Legacy DOCX files were not opened. The templates do not reproduce paper prose, abstracts, claims, findings, citations, reviewer comments, author or reviewer identities, correspondence, or journal-specific formatting.

No bundled publisher class/style/bibliography files, forms, logos, or other third-party assets are included. This component is released under the MIT License; see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). No third-party source material is included in this component.
