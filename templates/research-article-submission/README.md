# Research Article Submission Templates

Three reusable starting points for preparing a research article, responding to peer review, and writing a submission cover letter. They are intentionally publisher-neutral and use bracketed placeholders instead of source-paper content.

## Templates

- [`templates/article-manuscript-template.md`](templates/article-manuscript-template.md) — a flexible research-article outline, with reproducibility and disclosure prompts where relevant.
- [`templates/response-to-reviewers-template.md`](templates/response-to-reviewers-template.md) — a point-by-point revision response scaffold.
- [`templates/submission-cover-letter-template.md`](templates/submission-cover-letter-template.md) — a concise submission letter scaffold.

## Use

Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`, remove prompts that do not apply, and adapt the structure to the target venue's current instructions and the study design. These templates do not establish that any declaration is true, do not guarantee venue compliance, and are not a substitute for checking a journal or conference's official requirements. Keep editor and reviewer correspondence private; do not publish completed submissions or responses without authorization.
